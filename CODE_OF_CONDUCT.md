# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CampusStay is a course project. We treat teammates, reviewers, and anyone who tries the system with respect.

We expect members to:

- Give feedback about the work, and keep it specific
- Disagree in the open, then decide and move on
- Credit teammates for their contributions
- Ask for help early when a task is blocked

The following are not acceptable:

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks
- Sharing another person's private information
- Deliberately breaking the build or hiding failing tests
- Using the project data, demo accounts, or M-Pesa sandbox for anything outside the course

## Scope

This standard applies to the repository, pull requests, issues, chats, and the project demo.

## Reporting

Tell Member 1 (project manager and business analyst) what happened, when, and who was involved. If you cannot reach Member 1, tell the course instructor.

The project manager will listen to the people involved, keep the report limited to the people who need it, and agree a next step with the instructor when the issue is serious. A member may be asked to change a review comment, to hand a task to someone else, or to leave a discussion.
